Incident report: healthcare supply chain scenario
=================================================

Al-Noor Hospital Group reported on 3 March 2026 that ransomware had been deployed across
its clinical estate.

The intrusion originated with the group's managed service provider: attackers compromised
the MSP's remote management tooling and pushed a malicious update to 210 clinical
workstations. Obfuscated payloads executed through a signed binary proxy, and security
tooling was disabled on affected hosts before files were encrypted.

Patient scheduling and pharmacy dispensing systems were unavailable for 26 hours. The
health regulator was notified within the statutory window. C2 beaconing was observed to
91.219.236.10. Reported by Dr. Hanan Alqahtani, h.alqahtani@alnoor-health.sa, on host
CLIN-WS-0087.
