Incident report: engineering firm exchange webshell
===================================================

An unpatched on-premises Exchange server at Kinda Engineering Consultants was exploited
through CVE-2021-34473. The intruder planted a web shell, dumped passwords from memory
with Mimikatz and set up a scheduled task for persistence. The anomaly was spotted by
sysadmin Waleed Fathi at 172.16.5.20.
