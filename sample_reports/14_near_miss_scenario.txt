Incident report: near miss scenario
===================================

Sahm Capital Investment Bank contained a ransomware intrusion on 21 May 2026 before any
encryption occurred.

Initial access was a spearphishing link that harvested credentials from an employee in the
finance department. The attacker signed in with the valid account, ran encoded PowerShell,
and attempted to disable the EDR agent — which triggered a tamper-protection alert.
Attempted lateral movement over SMB shares was blocked by network segmentation, and an
attempt to delete shadow copies was blocked by application control.

No files were encrypted and no data was exfiltrated. Total time from first alert to full
containment was 12 minutes. Reported by Analyst Reem Alkhalifa,
r.alkhalifa@sahmcapital.sa, attacker source 178.62.44.19.
