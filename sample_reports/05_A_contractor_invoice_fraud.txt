Incident report: contractor invoice fraud
=========================================

Riyadh Towers Contracting lost SAR 1.2 million to invoice fraud. The accounts payable
clerk, Abdullah Al-Ghamdi, had entered his password on a phishing page that looked like
the Microsoft 365 sign-in. The attacker logged into his mailbox from 102.165.48.77,
created an inbox rule that moved every message from the supplier into the RSS folder, and
replied to an open invoice thread impersonating the vendor with updated banking details.
The payment was wired to the new bank account. Reported to finance director Lama Al-
Zahrani (lama.z@rtc-contracting.sa).
