Incident report: bank trojanised vendor update
==============================================

Bank of the Two Rivers installed a routine signed update for its branch queue-management
software from vendor QueueSoft. The update had been tampered with before release in the
vendor's build environment and carried a backdoor that beaconed to cdn-metrics-cloud.net
over HTTPS every 30 minutes. Analyst Salman Al-Fahad found the beacon in proxy logs on
host BR-QMS-17.
