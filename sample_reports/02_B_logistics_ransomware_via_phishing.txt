Incident report: logistics ransomware via phishing
==================================================

Gulf Freight Logistics' dispatch office lost access to its shared drives on a Thursday
night. Investigation showed that an employee, Omar Haddad, had followed a link in a fake
parcel-tracking email and entered his username and password on a spoofed login page. Using
that account from 91.219.236.18, the intruders launched obfuscated PowerShell scripts that
turned off the endpoint protection, then spread to other hosts through SMB file shares.
Before encryption they removed the Windows shadow copies. Every file share was encrypted
and a ransom note was left on each server. Reported by omar.haddad@gulffreight.com.
