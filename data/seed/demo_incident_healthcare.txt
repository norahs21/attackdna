INCIDENT REPORT — INTERNAL / RESTRICTED
Organisation: Al-Waha Regional Health Group
Reference: IR-2026-0417
Reported by: Layla Al-Otaibi, SOC Team Lead (layla.alotaibi@alwaha-health.example)
Escalated to: soc-oncall@alwaha-health.example, +966 55 123 4567

SUMMARY
On 14 April 2026 at 02:41 local time, the SOC at Al-Waha Regional Health Group
detected ransomware encrypting files across the radiology and patient-records
file servers. Two hospitals and four outpatient clinics were affected. Clinical
systems were unavailable for 31 hours and appointments were diverted to paper
workflows.

INITIAL ACCESS
The attacker authenticated to the remote access gateway (netscaler-edge01.alwaha-health.example,
10.44.12.8) without triggering multi-factor authentication. Forensics attributed this
to session token theft through the unpatched Citrix NetScaler appliance, exploiting
CVE-2023-4966. The appliance had been scheduled for patching in March but the change
window was deferred.

EXECUTION AND PERSISTENCE
Within forty minutes the attacker ran base64-encoded PowerShell on the jump host
(jmp-ops-02.alwaha-health.example, 10.44.20.15). A scheduled task named
"HealthSyncUpdate" was created on three servers to re-launch the loader after reboot.
The service account svc_backup, whose password had not been rotated since 2021, was
used for subsequent activity.

CREDENTIAL ACCESS AND LATERAL MOVEMENT
LSASS memory was dumped on the jump host. The harvested domain administrator
credentials were then used to move laterally over SMB to eighteen hosts in the
10.44.0.0/16 range, including the backup server bkp-prod-01.alwaha-health.example.

DEFENCE EVASION
The endpoint detection agent was stopped on nine servers using the vendor's own
uninstall utility with the credentials obtained above. Windows event logs were
cleared on the file servers.

EXFILTRATION
Approximately 340 GB of data, including patient identifiers and staff payroll
records, was uploaded to an external cloud storage service over HTTPS before
encryption began. Outbound traffic was observed to 185.199.47.203 on port 443.

IMPACT
Volume shadow copies were deleted on all affected servers and files were encrypted
with the extension .awh. A ransom note named RESTORE-FILES-AWH.txt demanded
payment of 900,000 USD in cryptocurrency, with a threat to publish the stolen
patient data within seven days. The attacker contacted the CFO directly at
f.alharbi@alwaha-health.example.

RESPONSE ACTIONS TAKEN
1. Isolated the affected VLANs and disconnected the two affected hospitals from the
   core network within 90 minutes of detection. Effective — stopped further encryption.
2. Disabled the svc_backup account and forced a domain-wide credential reset.
   Effective.
3. Patched the NetScaler appliance and rotated all session tokens. Effective — root cause.
4. Restored the radiology file server from immutable offline backups taken 11 April.
   Partially effective — 36 hours of records required manual re-entry.
5. Enabled tamper protection on all endpoint agents to prevent uninstallation.
   Effective — root cause fix.
6. Deployed detection for scheduled-task creation by non-administrative accounts.
   Effective.

NOTES
No ransom was paid. The incident was reported to the national regulator on
15 April 2026. Patient notification letters were issued by the legal team.
